Overview
About Days Inn Bismarck

Days Inn by Wyndham Bismarck is a full-service economy hotel conveniently located at 1300 East Capitol Avenue, Bismarck, North Dakota. Situated within easy reach of Interstate 94, the property offers guests a clean, comfortable base for exploring the state capital.

The hotel is part of the globally recognised Days Inn brand — a member of the Wyndham Hotels & Resorts family — and participates in the Wyndham Rewards loyalty programme, allowing guests to earn and redeem points on every stay.

Bismarck is North Dakota's state capital and second-largest city, home to the North Dakota State Capitol building, the Missouri River, and a vibrant local dining and arts scene. The hotel's central location puts all of these within minutes.
